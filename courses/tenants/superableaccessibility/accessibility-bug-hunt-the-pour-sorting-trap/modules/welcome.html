Welcome to the POUR Sorting Trap

This is a live, hands-on page — not a slide. Over the next few minutes you'll try three quick tests that anyone can run, regardless of how much accessibility or assistive tech experience you have.

The three tests

Keyboard only

Put your mouse or trackpad aside. Navigate using only Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, and Space.

200% browser zoom

Press Ctrl/Cmd + + a few times until your browser reports 200%. Ctrl/Cmd + 0 resets it.

Tab and look

Just watch where your focus indicator lands — and notice anything on the page it never lands on.

What you're about to see

The next page looks like an ordinary sorting activity about WCAG's four POUR principles. It's built to look completely normal to a sighted mouse user. Your job is simply to try the three tests above on it and notice what happens. We'll debrief together on the last page — no need to name the bugs out loud as you find them, just take note.

When you're ready, use the course menu to move on to the activity.
